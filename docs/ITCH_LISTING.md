# B.C.'s Quest for Tires · A Stone Age Adventure

**Short description:** One wheel. Big trouble. A prehistoric comic adventure, reimagined in Three.js.

Help Thor rescue his sweetheart from a hungry dinosaur. Hop the rocks, duck the branches, catch a lift from Dooky Bird, and put your trust in some deeply unreliable turtles.

This unofficial fan remake brings the C64 adventure into a new illustrated world: bold comic outlines, layered prehistoric scenery, expressive characters and newly synthesized music and sound effects.

- Nine chapters, from Sunstone Plains to the final cave.
- Turtle rivers, a bird-assisted lava crossing and a running cliff jump.
- Keyboard, touch controls and standard gamepad mappings.
- Modern buffered jumps or Classic direct inputs.
- Practice chapters, slow motion, collision-box display and optional invulnerability.
- Separate music, effects and ambience controls; reduced-motion setting.

**Controls:** arrows / A-D to move; Up / W / Space to jump; Down / S to duck; Shift + Left/Right to change speed; Enter to start; P / Escape to pause; M for sound; F for fullscreen.

**About this version:** v0.1.0 is a new interpretation with original illustrations and new physics/course spacing. It does not execute the C64 program. Physical slopes and alternating two-player mode are not implemented. This is an unofficial fan project; B.C., its characters and the original game belong to their respective rights holders.

Original comic: Johnny Hart. Original game: Sydney Development / Sierra On-Line. Rendering: Three.js.
